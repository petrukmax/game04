Mars Arachnid Defense

Two supply roads cross the red regolith. At their end stands First Bastion, the last human outpost on Mars. Build ten kinds of defenses, upgrade them through three tiers, time five tactical abilities and hold fifteen sectors against a swarm of armored arachnids, burrowers, flyers and brood tyrants.
